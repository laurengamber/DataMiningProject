\subsection*{Problem and Motivation}
In recent years, technology has advanced to the point where self-driving cars are becoming a viable alternative to human-driven cars. Public opinion remains divided on self-driving cars. Additionally, most people form their opinions on this topic by reading news articles. News articles are abundant, with multiple new sources writing multiple articles on the same topic over time. These data remain largely unstructured and vast; as a result, it is difficult for the public to identify themes across news sources. Thus, our project aims to identify hidden themes across news sources in articles about self-driving cars.      


\subsection*{Data and Availability}
Our proposed dataset is the "All the news" dataset, available as open-source on Kaggle. This dataset includes 2.79 million English news articles from January 2016 to April 2020. We filtered the 2.79 million news articles to 7,337 articles that discuss self-driving cars. We saved this subset as a new CSV file.

\subsection*{Proposed Approach}
We will be following the BERTopic Pipeline in Python.
\begin{itemize}

\item Data Preprocessing: Prepare the raw text by removing whitespace, lowercasing, and removing extra spaces.
 
\item Document Embeddings: using Sentence Transformers 

\item Dimensionality Reduction through Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection

\item Clustering through HDBSCAN

\item c-TF-IDF Representation

We will be using packages to complete this topic modeling pipeline. 

\end{itemize}

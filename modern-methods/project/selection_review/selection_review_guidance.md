## Rubric

- Semantic consistency: 1 = unrelated; 2 = mainly shared format or weak thematic relation; 3 = partially shared substantive theme; 4 = clear related themes with noticeable breadth; 5 = a strong shared central theme in all three sampled articles.
- Label specificity: 1 = generic/style words; 2 = weak subject hints; 3 = a broad domain; 4 = a clear subject or activity with some broad/company terms; 5 = a specific identifiable substantive theme.

Consistency evaluates the sampled article content; specificity evaluates what the existing keywords support, not an improved label we could invent afterward. Scores describe these representative samples, not all articles in a cluster.

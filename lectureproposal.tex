\section*{Lecture: Text Mining}

Our lecture group members are Laura Banaszewski, Mary Kate Carara, and Lauren Gamber.

\textbf{Title of Lecture: `Text Mining: Latent Dirichlet Allocation`.} Text data are collected in vast amounts and present unique challenges to be analyzed and processed. This lecture aims to explore those challenges and introduce one method of topic modeling, Latent Dirichlet Allocation, which uncovers themes within a collection of documents.

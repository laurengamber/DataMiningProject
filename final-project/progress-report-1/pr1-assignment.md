Progress Report 1: Paper Blueprint and Research Plan

Purpose

The purpose of the first progress report is to transform your approved project proposal into a detailed plan for a conference style research paper. You are not expected to have completed the analysis or obtained results at this stage. However, you should demonstrate that you have refined the problem, investigated relevant research, examined the data (or at least identified the data source), and developed a feasible analytical and evaluation plan.

This report will become the foundation of your final paper. You should organize it using the anticipated structure of that paper and provide enough detail under each section to show what the completed manuscript will contain.

Conference venue and format

Identify one conference that could reasonably be considered for your completed work. Explain briefly why the conference is appropriate for the project topic and contribution.

Unless another venue is approved by the instructor, use the current IEEE conference paper template. The proposed venue is used to establish a realistic audience and writing standard. You are not required to submit the final paper to the conference unless you prefer so.

Required content

Title and authors (5 p)
Provide a specific, informative working title and list all group members.

Proposed paper outline
Create a detailed outline of the anticipated paper. At minimum, include the following sections:

Abstract
Introduction
Related work
Data
Methodology
Experimental design
Results
Discussion
Limitations and responsible use considerations
Conclusion
References
You may modify this organization if another structure is more appropriate for your proposed conference.

Under each section and subsection, provide paragraph level notes describing:

The main claim, question, or purpose of the section
The evidence, analysis, or literature that will support it
Anticipated tables, figures, equations, or algorithms
Work that has been completed
Information or analysis that is still needed
Do not submit an outline consisting only of section titles. The outline should make the logic and anticipated content of the paper clear.

Introduction and research questions
Develop the proposal’s problem and motivation into a preliminary introduction. Clearly explain:

The problem being investigated
Why the problem is important
The limitation, gap, or practical need addressed by the project
The research questions or analytical objectives
The anticipated contribution of the study
The contribution does not need to be a new algorithm. It may involve a rigorous comparison, an adaptation of existing methods, analysis of a challenging dataset, evaluation under realistic conditions, or a meaningful application of advanced data mining methods.

Preliminary related work
Identify and organize the most relevant scholarly literature. The discussion should compare prior studies rather than provide a list of summaries. Explain how the proposed project relates to existing work and what distinguishes it.

Include a preliminary reference list containing credible and relevant scholarly sources. Students are responsible for reading and verifying every cited source.

Data understanding and initial audit
Provide a more detailed description of the proposed dataset than was included in the proposal. Address:

Data source and access status
Unit of observation
Relevant variables and target variable, if applicable
Dataset size and structure
Missing data, imbalance, duplicates, outliers, or measurement concerns
Privacy, licensing, or ethical considerations
Any preliminary descriptive statistics or visualizations
A feasible alternative if access to the preferred data remains uncertain
You should have obtained and inspected the data by this report unless the instructor has approved an exception.

Proposed methodology
Describe the methods you expect to investigate and explain why they fit the problem and data. Include:

A simple and meaningful baseline
The primary data mining methods
Anticipated preprocessing and feature construction
Important hyperparameters or design choices
Planned comparisons or ablation studies
Software, hardware, or external resources required
A list of algorithms is insufficient. Explain the role each method will play in answering the research question.

Evaluation and validation plan
Explain how the study will produce credible evidence. Address:

Performance metrics and why they are appropriate
Training, validation, and testing strategy
Measures to prevent data leakage
Treatment of temporal, spatial, subject level, or group dependence when relevant
Methods for addressing imbalance or unequal error costs
Planned robustness, sensitivity, or subgroup analyses
Criteria that will be used to interpret success
Project plan and responsibilities
Provide a schedule for completing the project. Identify major tasks, dependencies, anticipated risks, and contingency plans.

For a group project, state the planned responsibilities of each member. All students are expected to understand the complete project and contribute meaningfully to the technical work and manuscript.

 

Submission requirements

Submit one PDF per group.
Use the approved conference template.
The report should not exceed four pages, excluding references.
Figures and tables should be numbered, captioned, and referenced in the text or outline.
Include links to the dataset and code repository when sharing them is permitted.
Cite all datasets, software, papers, and external resources.
Clearly distinguish completed work from planned work.
Evaluation criteria

The report will be evaluated based on:

Clarity and significance of the research problem
Quality and specificity of the paper outline
Understanding of relevant literature
Evidence of data access and data understanding
Suitability and justification of the proposed methodology
Soundness of the evaluation plan
Feasibility of the completion plan
Responsiveness to prior feedback
Organization, clarity, and scholarly presentation
A detailed and realistic plan is more valuable than an ambitious plan that does not identify how credible evidence will be produced.

## Format and route details

**FLAIRS / ANLP:** This is the strongest first option to investigate, rather than an assertion that acceptance is likely. The ANLP page lists contextual representations, applied case studies, and evaluation among its interests. It contains stale edition links, but the current main conference dates independently confirm the January 22 mandatory abstract and February 1 paper deadlines. The main site also lists a March 15, 2027 poster-paper deadline (two pages plus references), offering a shorter contribution route. Deadlines are 23:59 Anywhere on Earth. Confirm the current template and category before preparing an actual submission. [ANLP call](https://sites.google.com/view/flairs-anlp/anlp-2027), [main conference dates](https://www.flairs-40.info/important-dates)

**ICSC:** Official submission instructions distinguish regular papers (8 pages), concise/industry papers (4 pages), and position papers (2 pages); limits include references. A separate poster-abstract route has different instructions, so do not equate a poster abstract with a short proceedings paper. A workshop could fit the semester schedule if its topic matches; the workshop's own call determines eligibility. The conference's shorter-paper routes do not establish that review is easy. [Submission instructions](https://www.semanticcomputing.org/submission), [workshop dates](https://www.semanticcomputing.org/dates)

**ICAART:** Regular papers represent completed and validated work; position papers can report work in progress. Accepted abstracts are presented but are not published in proceedings. The site lists December 3 deadlines for special sessions/workshops, but these are not a general extension of the main-paper deadline: inspect the specific call for topic fit and rules. [Official call and categories](https://icaart.scitevents.org/CallForPapers.aspx)

**Big Data:** The cited 2026 call specifies a ten-page limit including references. That is distinct from PR1's four-page allowance excluding references. A future main-track application paper would need a clear contribution and rigorous evaluation; do not justify it by labeling 7,337 articles as inherently large scale. [Official call](https://bigdataieee.org/BigData2026/calls/papers/)

## What would make the work ready for external review?

Across the shortlist, the practical priorities are the same:

1. Preserve the recovered R Markdown extraction rules and complete the human relevance audit. The source script is `data/Text Mining News Articles.Rmd`; full-source re-extraction has not been repeated.
2. Fit LDA and finalize BERTopic using documented criteria; preserve evidence supporting topic labels.
3. Complete independent human assessment and report disagreement, unassigned articles, unequal topic counts, and sensitivity to seeds and corpus decisions.
4. Make a substantive contribution: show what is learned about news coverage and which conclusions persist across methods, rather than only compare coherence values.
5. Replace pending literature-review notes with a verified comparative synthesis; select displays appropriate to the chosen page limit.

Before choosing an actual submission, check the current call, total registration/travel costs, presentation requirements, and applicable template. No registration prices, comparative acceptance probabilities, or future unpublished deadlines have been assumed here.

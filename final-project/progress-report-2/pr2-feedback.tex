My main concern is that the team has made good progress implementing and evaluating the algorithms. In next report I expect to see answers to your research question like what themes appear in autonomous vehicle coverage, how those themes differ across publications, and whether LDA and BERTopic lead to different conclusions. A useful minimum would be a table showing each major topic’s label, keywords, size, and representative articles, followed by an initial comparison across publications. 

Another concern is that you developed vocabulary and configuration choices using the full corpus, then proposed setting aside 20% for testing. Those articles have already influenced development decisions, even if the models are subsequently refitted on the remaining 80%. You can describe it as an exploratory robustness check, disclose the earlier full corpus development, and avoid claiming a completely untouched evaluation.
